R00K Steward

A personal inbox and calendar helper that runs on one person's own PC.

What it is

R00K Steward is part of R00K, a private AI app made by one person for their own use. It is not a public service: nobody else can sign up, and it is not sold or shared.

What it does

  • Reads new mail in the owner's own Gmail accounts.
  • Adds labels, such as Bills, Needs reply or Receipts.
  • Writes draft replies into the owner's Gmail Drafts folder. It never sends mail by itself.
  • Reads the owner's Google Calendar, and adds an event only when the owner says yes.
  • Archives, deletes or unsubscribes only when the owner says yes, or by a rule the owner made.

Google permissions it asks for

  • Gmail (read, label, draft): gmail.modify
  • Calendar events: calendar.events
